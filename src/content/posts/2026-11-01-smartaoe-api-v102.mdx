SmartAoE v1.0.2 adds one feature asked for by a player: areas of effect that stop at walls. Until now a fireball reached every token inside its radius, even those in the next room. There are no breaking changes, and walls are still ignored unless a macro asks for them.

## Change Log

### Added

- **`--blockedBy|walls`** stops an AoE at dynamic lighting walls (Jumpgate and Legacy) and at closed doors and windows. A square is left out when no straight line from the point of origin reaches it past a wall, as the D&D 5e rules say. Its centre and four points near its corners are tried, so a square seen through a doorway still counts. Open doors and windows let the AoE through, and a caster standing against a wall isn't blocked by it.
- **`--blockedBy|walls, spread`** also lets the AoE flow round corners from square to square, as far as its reach, as the 2014 _Fireball_ does.

For example, a fireball that stops at walls but spreads round corners, as the 2014 spell does (use plain `walls` for the 2024 rule):

```
!smartaoe {{
  --aoeType|circle, float
  --radius|20ft
  --blockedBy|walls, spread
  --dc|@{selected|spell_save_dc}
  --saveFormula|5eDEX
  --damageFormula1|[[8d6]]
  --damageType1|fire
  --fx|burn-fire
}}
```

Walls are checked when the AoE is drawn, that is when the control token moves. After opening or closing a door, nudge the control token so the AoE is drawn again before you trigger it.

- Roll20 One-Click Installer (once Roll20 merges v1.0.2; until then One-Click installs v1.0.0).
- [GitHub](https://github.com/steverobertsuk/roll20-api-scripts/tree/SmartAoE-v1.0.2/SmartAoE)
- [Download and install manually via GitHub](https://github.com/steverobertsuk/roll20-api-scripts/releases/download/SmartAoE-v1.0.2/SmartAoE_v1.0.2.zip).

SmartAoE requires the **libInline** mod, which the One-Click installer adds automatically.
