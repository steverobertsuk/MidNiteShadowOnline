Customizable Roll Listener v2.1.0 comes from ideas by Pak on Discord, who wanted a listener for sword attacks on the D&D 2024 sheet. Matching on text alone also caught other cards that mentioned a sword, so listeners can now check a roll template's fields directly and skip messages containing certain text. There are no breaking changes.

## Change Log

### Added

- **`--fields`** filters on roll template fields. For example, `--fields subcategory=Attack && title~sword` triggers only on D&D 2024 attack cards for swords. `=` and `!=` compare the whole value, `~` and `!~` look for text in it (a regular expression with `--regex on`), and case is ignored. The inspector lists each message's fields and says when a field doesn't match.
- **`--exclude`** stops a listener when any of its pieces of text appears, such as `--text sword --exclude dagger`. It's searched like `--text`, and the inspector says when excluded text was found.

```
!crl new Sword Attacks --fields subcategory=Attack && title~sword --commands /em swings a sword!
```

### Changed

- The README and Help handout explain that `success` and `fail` need a target written into the roll, such as `1d20>15`, so they never trigger on D&D sheet attacks, saves and checks. Use `--total` for an AC or DC instead.
- They also explain that `--text` searches the raw content the inspector shows, which for a Beacon card is its HTML.
- The inspector shortens embedded images in the raw content to their type. The weapon icon on a D&D 2024 attack card used up most of the 3,000 characters shown, hiding attributes such as `data-rollSubcategory`.
