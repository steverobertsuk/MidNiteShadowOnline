Guards on patrol, a dog in its pen, a party following its leader: TokenController moves tokens along routes for you, so you don't have to drag them every turn. This video goes through every feature of version 5.0.1.

## What the video covers

It starts with a guard walking his rounds, the party following its leader in single file, and Reset sending the guard home. Then it opens the menu and the Command Deck from the Journal, starts the ready-made example routes and sets the patrol speed. It starts a route on a token, writes a new one with a step code, and stops and resets a patrol from its route card. A drawn pen becomes a bounce area for a dog to wander in. The party follows its leader a square at a time and after a long drag. A locked token holds still, and carries on along its route when it is unlocked. Combat On and the Turn Order pause every patrol, Stop All brings them all to a stop, and finally the Command Deck and Help switch to French and back.
