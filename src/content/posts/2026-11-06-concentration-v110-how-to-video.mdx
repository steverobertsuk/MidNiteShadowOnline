Every hit on a concentrating spellcaster needs a saving throw, and it is the easiest thing in the game to forget. Concentration rolls it for you, and this video goes through every feature of version 1.1.0.

## What the video covers

It starts with the Command Deck handout and the settings menu, marks a caster with a command and switches spells, then casts a concentration spell from the D&D 2024 sheet so the marker goes on by itself. It shows the save being rolled when the caster takes damage, dropping to 0 hit points, advantage on the save, rolling saves yourself from a reminder, who sees the messages, the status card, ending concentration, and the 24 languages.
