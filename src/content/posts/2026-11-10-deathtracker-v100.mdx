Who's bloodied, who's dying, and how many death saves have they made? **DeathTracker** watches hit points and keeps track for you, straight from the character sheet.

Version 1.0.0 is a full rewrite of Robin Kuiper's DeathTracker 0.1.8, which had not changed since 2018. It works in Jumpgate and reads death saves from the D&D 2024 sheet. Your 0.1 settings carry over and `!dead` still works, and a new [five-minute How-To video](/posts/deathtracker-v100-how-to-video/) shows every feature.

## Change Log

### Fixed

- Clearing a token's hit point bar marked it dead (an empty bar was read as 0). Empty bars, and bars that don't hold a number, are now left alone.
- A token with no maximum hit points got a broken tint (`#NaNNaNNaN`).
- Tokens controlled by more than one player, or by "all", were wrongly treated as NPCs or PCs.
- Only Roll20's old built-in markers could be chosen. Any marker in the game now works, including custom marker sets and the D&D 2024 sheet's condition markers.
- Changing the hit point bar no longer says the sandbox needs a restart.
- At full hit points the tint no longer wipes a tint set by the GM or another Mod.
- Settings typed in chat are checked, and bad values are refused with a message.
- One Mod's failing `ObserveTokenChange` handler no longer stops DeathTracker and the other handlers.

### Added

- **Death saves** for player characters: **Dying** at 0, **Stable** after three successes, **Dead** after three failures. They're read from the D&D 2024 sheet as boxes are ticked or saves rolled, from checkbox fields on the 2014 sheet and others, or from a **DeathSaves** token action.
- Saves are counted from the drop to 0, and the sheet's boxes are cleared at the drop and on healing. On the D&D 2024 sheet, the death save boxes are opened when a PC drops to 0 from the token bar.
- **Who is a PC**: player-controlled tokens or characters, or characters without an NPC sheet, plus **Make PC** and **Make NPC** per character.
- A **Bloodied** threshold setting (50% by default).
- **When a token dies** (each off by default): move it to the GM layer or the map layer, and remove it from the turn order. **Restore** puts it back, with its initiative.
- A **massive damage** option for PCs, and announcements to the GM and the PC's players.
- **Who handles what**, shared with [Aura/Tint HealthColors 2.5](/posts/aura-tint-healthcolors-api-v250/) and later: choose DeathTracker, HealthColors or Off for death saves, health colours and hurt/heal effects.
- A chat menu, a settings card, **DeathTracker - Help** and **DeathTracker - Command Deck** handouts with the DeathTracker icon in the Journal, One-Click options for every setting, and 24 languages.
- An API for other Mods: `check`, `getStatus`, `onStatusChange`, `isManagingDeathSaves` and `ownersChanged`, alongside 0.1's `ObserveTokenChange`.

```
!deathtracker
!deathtracker stable
!deathtracker restore --ids <token id>
```

### Changed

- The main command is `!deathtracker` (also `!dt`); `!dead`, `!dead config key|value` and `!dead reset` still work.
- The PC Unconscious marker is now the Dying marker.

## Where to install the mod from?

- Roll20 One-Click Installer (once Roll20 merges v1.0.0; until then One-Click installs v0.1.8).
- [GitHub](https://github.com/steverobertsuk/roll20-api-scripts/tree/DeathTracker-v1.0.0/DeathTracker)
- [Download and install manually via GitHub](https://github.com/steverobertsuk/roll20-api-scripts/releases/download/DeathTracker-v1.0.0/DeathTracker_v1.0.0.zip).

If 0.1.8 is installed, replace it rather than running both. DeathTracker works in Jumpgate and Legacy games and has no dependencies.
