A spell that swaps two creatures, or a monster that trades places with its victim: dragging two tokens past each other is fiddly. Swap Token Positions swaps them in one command, with effects, and this video goes through every feature of version 2.1.0.

## What the video covers

It starts with the help command and a basic swap, then shows the portal, lightning and fire presets, mixing your own origin, travel and destination effects, and skipping them all. It swaps tokens by name, saves your effects as defaults, lets players swap their own tokens, builds an Echo Knight swap macro, and finishes with the error messages and resetting the settings.
