I'm pleased to announce the release of **TaskAutomation**, a new Roll20 Mod that lets Game Masters automate the little jobs that pile up during a session, without writing any JavaScript.

Cycle a multi-sided token through a montage, remind a player that their torch is guttering, mark a token as dead when its hit points reach zero, ping the token whose turn it is, fade the music while a door creaks open, or remind the whole table every Friday that the session is about to start. Each of these is a **task**: a short description of what should happen, and when, written as data rather than code.

## Why another automation Mod?

Most timed effects in Roll20 Mods rely on a scattering of `setTimeout` calls, and they share the same weaknesses. When the API sandbox restarts, everything that was waiting is lost. When several things are waiting at once, the game carries a timer for each of them. And nobody can easily see what is due to happen next.

TaskAutomation was built around those problems:

- **One timer for everything.** All waiting work sits in a single queue with one timer, so an idle game costs nothing.
- **Tasks survive restarts.** A run carries on from the step it had reached, on its original deadline.
- **Nothing happens twice by accident.** If the sandbox stopped part-way through sending a message or changing a token, TaskAutomation checks what actually happened. When it can't be sure, it asks you rather than guessing.
- **Missed work is handled your way.** Times that passed while the sandbox was off can be skipped, run once, caught up a little at a time, sent to you for a decision or dropped, and they are always reported.

## What tasks can do

- **Steps**: wait for a time or until a moment, wait for an event with a timeout, branch on a condition, loop, call other tasks and run branches in parallel.
- **Actions**: chat messages, token properties and sides, status markers, attributes, pings, visual effects, jukebox tracks, and commands to other Mods.
- **Triggers**: a button or command, a set time, an interval, a daily or weekly time, or a change to tokens, attributes, the turn order, the player page, players or signals.

A GM-only **Dashboard** handout shows what is running, waiting, paused or missed, with buttons to pause, resume and cancel runs and to decide anything that needs your attention. A GM-only **Help** handout lists every command, step and trigger.

## Ready-made examples

You don't need to write a task to get started. TaskAutomation comes with templates you can add, try and adapt:

- **Adventure montage**: cycle up to five multi-sided tokens through their sides.
- **Torch reminder**: warn after 50 minutes and put the torch out after 10 more.
- **HP threshold**: mark a token dead and tell the GM when bar 1 drops to 0.
- **Turn response**: ping the token whose turn it is.
- **Parallel scene**: fade a jukebox track while a door opens, then tidy up.
- **Session reminder**: every Friday at 19:00, at the UTC offset you choose.
- **Other-Mod command**: send a TokenMod command for the selected token.
- **Door sequence**: wait up to a minute for the lever to be pulled.

## Safe by default

Everything is GM-only unless you choose otherwise. A task can be shared with named players, who can then use it only on tokens they control, and a player's run stops for your review if they lose control of its token part-way through. An emergency **Stop all** cancels every run and holds new starts until you allow them again.
