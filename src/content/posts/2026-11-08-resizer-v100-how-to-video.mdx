Maps that came in at the wrong size, tokens that need to be Large, a whole page that needs to be bigger: Resizer does all of it, and moves the walls with the map. This video goes through every feature of version 1.0.0.

## What the video covers

It starts with a beach map imported at the wrong size, fitted to the grid in one go, and a page zoomed to twice the size with its walls, door, window and labels. Then it opens the Command Deck handout and the `!resizer` menu, makes a goblin Large and sets an exact size. It calibrates the beach map from a box drawn over four by two of its squares, zooms a page and undoes it, and keeps the tokens one square big. It zooms the Docks and the Market together, undoes both with one click from the history, goes through the settings and the 0.0.9 commands, and finishes with the 24 languages.
