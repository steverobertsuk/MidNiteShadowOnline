InspirationTracker keeps inspiration on the character sheet, the token and the chat in step: D&D's Inspiration and Heroic Inspiration, or points pools for any game, such as luck or hero points. This video goes through every feature of version 1.0.0.

## What the video covers

It starts with a quick look at what the Mod does: Kildrak lands the finishing blow and the GM gives him Heroic Inspiration, Seraphina's player spends hers from the chat, and the GM gets a whisper with an Undo button.

Then it opens the Command Deck handout and the `!inspiration` menu, clicks the Inspiration button on a D&D 2024 sheet, and adds the players' token action. A player spends from their own card and the GM undoes it, and Seraphina, who already has Heroic Inspiration, passes it on to Kildrak. The Party card shows who has it, TokenMod gives and spends it through the marker, and the settings change the marker, turn it off with None, and set the wording.

The longest chapter is points pools: Tracking is changed to Points pool, Kildrak gains ten points, and the Party card shows where they are kept on the D&D 2024 sheet and in a resource box on the 2014 sheet. It sets the cost of a +1, gives the pool a marker of its own, spends points with Boost and adds a second pool called Luck. The video finishes by switching the language to French and back.
