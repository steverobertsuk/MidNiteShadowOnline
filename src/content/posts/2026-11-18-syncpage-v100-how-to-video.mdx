Roll20 can't hide a token from only some of your players. SyncPage keeps copies of a map in step, so each group of players can have its own copy and you only change what each page hides. This video goes through every feature of version 1.0.0.

## What the video covers

It starts with why you'd want SyncPage: a guard has drunk a potion of invisibility, the cleric can see him and Kildrak can't, and the Lab Room map has three copies to show that. Kildrak's player moves to his own copy and the guard is hidden there only, with the GM's and the player's screens side by side: the mastiff moves for both, the guard only for the GM.

Then it opens the `!syncpage` menu, the Help handout and the Command Deck, makes and deletes a sync group, drags a hill giant and checks its copies with Status, and hides the mastiff on one page. It saves and restores the players' layout, previews a repair, undoes a delete on every page, sets deletes to Ask, and finishes by switching the language to French and back.

New to splitting the party in Roll20? The video points to [Nick Olivo's video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mBxSJfmGjlQ&t=229s), which shows how.
