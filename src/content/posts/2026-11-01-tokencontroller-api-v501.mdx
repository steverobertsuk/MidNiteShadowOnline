TokenController v5.0.1 comes from feedback by a player who tried v5.0.0: dead guards kept marching after a fight, patrols walked straight through walls, ScriptCards couldn't tell TokenController which tokens to move, and there was no way to let a token wander around a room. All four are now covered. There are no breaking changes, and the new settings are off until you turn them on.

## Change Log

### Fixed

- **Commands sent by other Mods** (for example a ScriptCards `--@tc|` call) were refused with "Only the GM can use TokenController", because Roll20 sends them from the player "API". They are now accepted, and their replies are whispered to the GM.

### Added

- **Stop Markers** setting: a list of status markers separated by semicolons, for example `!tokencontroller set stopMarkers dead;red`. A patrolling token that gets one leaves its route, so dead guards stop marching after a fight. **Stop Marker Action** chooses whether it stays where it is or goes back to where it started. Marked tokens stop following, followers of a marked leader stop too, and marked tokens can't be started. Markers are checked every quarter of a second, even while patrols are paused for combat, so markers added by other Mods such as TokenMod count.
- **`--ids`** for `start`, `stop`, `reset`, `lock`, `unlock` and `remove`: act on the tokens with the given IDs (separated by semicolons, commas or spaces) instead of the selection, so other Mods can control tokens. For example, `!tc stop --ids -Mabc123;-Mdef456`. `remove --ids` takes tokens off their routes and never removes a route.
- **Walls** setting and per-route `walls` option: patrols can wait at, or turn back from, dynamic lighting walls and closed doors and windows, in Jumpgate and Legacy, instead of walking through them. A patrol waiting at a door carries on when a player opens it, and followers wait rather than cross a wall. A route set to `ignore` still walks through walls, for ghosts.
  - **Wall Check** chooses whether only a token's centre or its whole width must stay clear, so a large token can't squeeze through a narrow gap.
  - **One-Way Walls** and **See-Through Walls** choose whether those kinds of wall block.
- **Bounce areas** (`bounce <name>` and a **Bounce Area** button): draw a closed shape around a room or a field and select it. Tokens started on the route wander inside it, travelling in straight lines and bouncing off its edges and any walls like a ball in Pong, turning a little at random each time so their path doesn't repeat. It works on every grid type and on gridless pages.
