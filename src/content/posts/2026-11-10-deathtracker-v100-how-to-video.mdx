Who's bloodied, who's dying, and how many death saves have they made? DeathTracker watches hit points and keeps track for you. This video goes through every feature of version 1.0.0.

## What the video covers

It starts with a goblin bloodied then killed, and Kildrak, a player character, dropping to 0 and becoming stable after three death saves on his D&D 2024 sheet. Then it opens the Command Deck handout and the `!deathtracker` menu, and changes the Bloodied threshold. A player character fails three death saves and is brought back with Revivify, another is told she's dying on her player's screen and is stabilised from the menu, and a PC is made an NPC. A goblin dies and leaves the map and the turn order, then is restored. It finishes with the D&D sheet's Unconscious marker for dying, and the 24 languages.
