Hex crawls are about not knowing what's round the next corner, but a virtual tabletop shows the whole map from the start. Hexploration covers a hex map with hexes and uncovers it hex by hex as the party explores. This video goes through every feature of version 2.0.3.

## What the video covers

It starts with a village hidden under hexes in one click, and a player's token clearing them as she walks. Then it opens the Help and Command Deck handouts and the `!hexploration` menu, marks a hidden location, and sets up mountains and woods that only clear when a token walks in. It covers the map with Fill, Inverse Fill and Undo Fill, follows the player's view as the party explores, and shows hidden monsters staying hidden until their hex is uncovered. It goes through Sight Distance, Line of Sight and announcements, Glimpse mode, the GM tools (Name Hex, Reveal, Conceal, Explore and Status), per-page settings, Rescan, Reveal Page and Reset Page, and finishes with the 24 languages.
