Customizable Roll Listener v2.0.1 is a small fix, found while working on feedback for TokenController: CRL refused commands sent by other Mods. There are no breaking changes.

## Change Log

### Fixed

- **Commands sent by other Mods**, such as a ScriptCards `--@crl|` call, are no longer refused with "Only a GM can use Customizable Roll Listener". Roll20 sends them from the player "API", which isn't a GM. They now work, and their replies are whispered to the GM.

## Where to install the mod from?

- Roll20 One-Click Installer (once Roll20 merges v2.0.1; until then One-Click installs v1.1).
- [GitHub](https://github.com/steverobertsuk/roll20-api-scripts/tree/CustomizableRollListener-v2.0.1/Customizable-Roll-Listener)
- [Download and install manually via GitHub](https://github.com/steverobertsuk/roll20-api-scripts/releases/download/CustomizableRollListener-v2.0.1/CRL_v2.0.1.zip).

Customizable Roll Listener has no dependencies.
