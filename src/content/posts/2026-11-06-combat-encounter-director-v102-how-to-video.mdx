Your encounter was built for four players and only two turned up. Combat Encounter Director rebalances the fight at the table, and this video goes through every feature of version 1.0.2.

## What the video covers

It starts with the quick menu and the Command Deck handout, then scales a group of monsters for the party size and shows the Boss and Minion presets, with the Preset Reference that explains what each one changes. After that come the Status handout, reinforcements kept hidden on the GM layer until you reveal them, a hidden ambush, saved positions, saving and loading a whole encounter, undoing your changes, and choosing one of the 24 languages.
