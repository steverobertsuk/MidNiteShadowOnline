A sound on a critical hit, a table roll on a fumble, a trap that springs when someone says the word: Customizable Roll Listener watches chat and runs your commands when a roll or message matches. This video goes through every feature of version 2.1.0.

## What the video covers

It starts with a trapped chest that springs when a player says "open the chest", then opens the Command Deck and uses Inspect Next Message to show exactly what CRL reads in a message. It builds a critical hit listener that calls out who rolled and the total, and a fumble from an inline roll in ordinary chat. It filters on words while leaving others out, reacts to a roll total and to success on Roll20's target rolls, and shows players' rolls setting listeners off. Then it edits a listener, adds a cooldown, disables and tests it, deletes it, pauses every listener with Listening Off, and finishes by switching the Command Deck to French and back.
