Rain on the docks, fog in the swamp, embers by the hearth, and a glyph that bursts into sparks when someone steps on it: PageFX fills part of your map with effects that keep going, on every page your players are on. This video goes through every feature of version 2.0.0.

## What the video covers

It starts with heavy rain over the docks from one click on the Command Deck, then opens the menu and gives tokens presets from the four packs, setting each effect's area with its aura. It builds effects of its own from a built-in effect in a chosen colour, with its timing, an extra layer and a custom FX definition, then turns effects off, pauses them, changes their intensity and uses Pause All and Resume All. It moves a player to a page of their own to show that page's effects running for them, keeps the rain out of a building with a no-effect area, and sets up a glyph that fires when a token steps into it and a storm that breaks when a new round of combat begins. It finishes with how version 1 tokens keep working, and switches the Command Deck to French and back.
