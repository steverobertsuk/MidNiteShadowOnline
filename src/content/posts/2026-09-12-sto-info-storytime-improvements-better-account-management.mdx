### Account & Captain organisation

Managing players with larger collections of Accounts and Captains is now easier.

- Added sorting and filtering controls to Account lists.
- Accounts can now be pinned, keeping the Accounts you use most prominently positioned.
- Added sorting and filtering to Captain lists within an Account.
- Captains can now also be pinned, with pinned Captains appearing ahead of other Captains when lists are sorted.
- Captains can be pinned or unpinned directly from their Captain page.
- Account lists can be sorted using information such as handle, platform, launcher and creation date.
- Captain lists can be sorted using information including name, level, species, faction, class and creation date.
- Improved Account and Captain cards to better support the new organisation controls.
- Public Account lists within the Galactic Personnel Registry also benefit from improved sorting and filtering.

### STO Storytime - Markdown preview

Writing formatted Storytime content is now considerably easier.

- Added an Edit / Preview facility to Storytime Markdown editors.
- Creators can preview a Chapter while writing it to see how its formatting will appear to readers before saving or publishing.
- Preview is also available when writing Story descriptions, Arc descriptions and Character biographies.
- Preview uses the same formatting system as published Storytime content, providing a much closer representation of the finished result.

### STO Storytime - Additional formatting

STO Info now provides two additional formatting options alongside standard Markdown.

- `{indent}` can be used at the beginning of a paragraph to indent its first line.
- `{spacer}` can be placed on its own line to add intentional vertical space between sections of content.
- The new formatting options work consistently across Storytime, News and other STO Info content that supports Markdown.
- The Markdown reference and Help information have been updated to explain the STO Info-specific formatting options.

### STO Storytime - Story management

Creators now have greater control over their Storytime content.

- Stories, Chapters and Arcs can now be deleted directly through Storytime.
- Published content must first be unpublished before it can be deleted, helping prevent accidental removal of content currently available to readers.
- Added confirmation prompts before destructive actions throughout Storytime.
- Confirmation messages explain what will be removed and, where appropriate, what will be retained.
- Additional confirmation protection has been added when removing Characters, collaborators, Chapter media, comments, reading lists, reading-list entries, tags, Spotlight entries and Stories from Arcs.

### STO Storytime - Crew Credits

Story creators can now properly manage the people credited for contributing to their work.

- Added a new Credits area to Story management.
- Creators can add, update and remove Crew credits.
- Credits can recognise contributions to an entire Story, individual Chapters or Characters.
- Contributions covering a range of Chapters can also be recorded.
- STO Info can help find community members by username when adding a credit.
- Published Story credits now clearly display the username of the person being credited.
- Credits remain separate from collaboration permissions - crediting somebody recognises their contribution but does not give them editing access to the Story.

### Other improvements

- Improved the presentation and behaviour of several Storytime management controls.
- Improved accessibility information for interactive controls, including pinned states.
- Improved the consistency of Storytime collaboration and management screens.
- Updated underlying application components and dependencies to their latest supported maintenance releases.
- Various behind-the-scenes reliability, testing, security and deployment improvements.
