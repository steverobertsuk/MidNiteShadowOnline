Hexploration v2.0.3 is a small fix, found while working on feedback for TokenController: Hexploration ignored commands sent by other Mods. There are no breaking changes.

## Change Log

### Fixed

- **Commands sent by other Mods**, such as a ScriptCards `--@hexploration|` call, are no longer ignored. Roll20 sends them from the player "API", which isn't a GM, so Hexploration dropped them without a word. They now work, and their replies are whispered to the GM.
