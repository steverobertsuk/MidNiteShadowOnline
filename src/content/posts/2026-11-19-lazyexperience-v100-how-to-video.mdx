LazyExperience collects the experience points a party earns during a session, splits them fairly and writes them to the character sheets. This video goes through every feature of version 1.0.0.

## What the video covers

It starts with a quick look at what the Mod does: the party fights four goblins, LazyExperience asks to add each one's fifty XP as it drops to zero hit points, and End Session splits the hundred XP between the four characters and writes it to their D&D 2024 sheets.

Then it opens the Command Deck handout and the `!lazyxp` menu, and shows the party: Roll20's own Party Member characters, with a half share for Orkira. It adds two hundred XP for rescuing the miller, gives Atala a bonus for a clever plan, and marks Kildrak absent. Damage typed into a goblin's hit point bubble brings up the defeated prompt, where Other Amount makes a goblin that was only chased off worth twenty-five, and Undo takes an award back.

Ending the session pays everyone and announces the level-ups, Seraphina's player gets a whisper of what she earned, `!lazyxp show` reads everyone's XP from their sheets, and the Session Log handout keeps the record. The video finishes with the settings, the game systems it supports, and switching the language to French.
