Who is grappled, who is frightened, and for how many more rounds? Condition Tracker puts every condition in the Turn Order, and this video goes through every feature of version 1.2.0.

## What the video covers

It starts with the menu in chat, the Help and Reference handout, the GM macros and what the players see. Then it uses the wizard to pick a condition, its source, its target and how long it lasts, puts one condition on several tokens, uses the Help preset for advantage on the next attack, and shows reports and removal, what happens when a creature drops to zero, the settings, and the 24 languages.
