In the middle of a fight, nobody wants to open every sheet to see who is hurt. Aura/Tint HealthColors shows each token’s health as a colour you can read at a glance, and this video goes through every feature of version 2.4.0.

## What the video covers

It starts with the menu in chat and the Help and Reference handout, connects the health bar for linked player characters and unlinked monsters, and shows what happens as damage and healing come in. Then it works through the aura’s radius, all six shapes, opacity, tinting the token instead, the default and colour-blind palettes, showing health only below a percentage, hurt and heal effects, death save markers from the D&D 2024 sheet, and the remaining settings.
