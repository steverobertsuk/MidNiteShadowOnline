Which goblins did that fireball actually catch? SmartAoE draws areas of effect on the grid, rolls the saves and applies the damage, and this video goes through every feature and every parameter of version 1.0.3.

## What the video covers

It starts with the help command and the Help & Reference handout, casts an area and aims it by dragging the reticle, then triggers it so every creature inside rolls its save, and applies the damage from the results card. It turns a cone round its caster, applies damage and condition markers automatically, stops an area at walls and closed doors, and finishes with an example of every parameter.
