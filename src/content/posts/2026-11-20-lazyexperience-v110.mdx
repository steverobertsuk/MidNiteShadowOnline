LazyExperience v1.1.0 started with a question on Reddit the day after [v1.0.0](/posts/lazyexperience-v100/) came out. Pathfinder 1E has three XP tracks, fast, medium and slow, and some GMs mix them: fast for the first few levels, medium for the middle of the campaign and slow after that. Could the Mod follow that, mid-game?

It couldn't, and checking why turned up a bug. Version 1.1.0 adds a **Pathfinder 1E track** setting that does exactly that, and fixes the bug.

## Change Log

### Added

- The **Pathfinder 1E track** setting: fast, medium or slow, or tracks that change by level, such as `fast 1-3, medium 4-13, slow 14+`. A character advances on the track for the level they are on now: in that example, a level 4 character needs 15,000 XP for level 5 (medium), and a level 14 character needs 955,000 for level 15 (slow).
- After each award, the XP for the next level is written to the sheet's next-level box (the number after the slash beside XP), so the sheet and the level-up notices agree.
- **Sheet box as typed** (`sheet`) reads the next-level box as the GM types it and leaves it alone.
- The settings card shows the track, such as "Fast 1–3 · Medium 4–13 · Slow 14+", with a **Mixed** button for tracks by level. There is a One-Click option too, and the new text is in all 24 languages.

```
!lazyxp set pf1Track fast
!lazyxp set pf1Track fast 1-3, medium 4-13, slow 14+
!lazyxp set pf1Track sheet
```

### Changed

- On the Pathfinder 1E sheets the Mod now writes the next-level box, from the medium track unless you choose another. If you type that box yourself, set **Pathfinder 1E track** to **Sheet box as typed**.
- `!lazyxp show` uses the same XP for the next level as the level-up notices.

### Fixed

- The next-level boxes on the Pathfinder 1E, Pathfinder Community and Starfinder sheets were never read. Roll20 keeps a box like `xp_max` as the second value of the `xp` attribute, not as an attribute of its own, so version 1.0.0 always fell back to the medium track (and Starfinder's own table).

- [GitHub](https://github.com/steverobertsuk/roll20-api-scripts/tree/LazyExperience-v1.1.0/LazyExperience)
- [Download and install manually via GitHub](https://github.com/steverobertsuk/roll20-api-scripts/releases/download/LazyExperience-v1.1.0/LazyExperience_v1.1.0.zip).

Replace v1.0.0 or 0.1.14 rather than running both. Your settings and the session's XP are kept.
