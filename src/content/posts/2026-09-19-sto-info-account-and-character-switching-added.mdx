Hi everyone 👋

This update makes it much faster to switch between your Star Trek Online accounts and Captains, especially for players who manage multiple Captains across different accounts.

### Quick Account & Captain switching

A new Quick Switcher has been added to the STO Info dashboard.

Instead of returning to Your Accounts each time you want to view another Captain, you can now open the switcher and jump directly to any STO account or Captain.

The switcher:

- shows all of your STO accounts and Captains together in one place;
- allows you to search for the account or Captain you want quickly;
- keeps pinned accounts and Captains at the top of the list;
- shows platform, launcher, faction and Captain imagery to make entries easier to identify;
- clearly highlights the account or Captain you are currently viewing; and
- allows you to switch directly between Captains belonging to different STO accounts.

The Quick Switcher is available from Your Accounts, individual Account and Captain pages, and Endeavours.

### Other improvements

The version information shown in the STO Info footer has been simplified. When the frontend and backend are running the same version, a single version number is now displayed.

The STO Info Roadmap has also been updated to reflect recently completed work and the next features currently in development.

This release also includes behind-the-scenes monitoring, maintenance, and dependency updates to support STO Info's continued stability and development.
